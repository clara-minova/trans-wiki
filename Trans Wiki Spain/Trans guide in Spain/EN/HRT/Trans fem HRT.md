
# Estradiol
In Spain exist 2 types of commercialized drugs for trans fem HRT:
1) pills
2) transdermal

## Pills
1) PROGYNOVA 1mg - estradiol valerate
2) ESTRADIOL MERIESTRA 2 MG - estradiol hemihydrate
3) Climen - combined drug estradiol valerate + cyproterone
   
Some doctors can make magistral receta for pharmacy to prepare "custom" pills specifically for you, unusual, but often happens in Barcelona Transit
   
## Transdermal
1) Lenzetto spray - cost is regulated and covered by SS, but currently experience supply shortage in Spain
2) Estradot patches - cost is regulated and covered by SS
3) Oestraclin gel - not covered by SS and cost is not regulated

## Injections
There are **no commercialized** estradiol injection drugs on the market in Spain, and doctors have general recommendation to avoid their usage, especially in public system. But it's possible to get custom manufactured by a pharmacy lab via "**magistral receta**", to get this prescription you would need to find a doctor who is willing to make such prescription for you.

# Anti androgens
There is many drugs on the market, including DHT blockers. Because of this I going to describe only main drugs usually prescribed in for trans fem HRT.

1) Androcur 50mg pills - one of most common choice. Cost is regulated and covered by SS. Easy to get from family doctor if you have medical report stating it as you active treatment.
2) Triptorelin injections - also common blocker, exist in 3 different dosages for 1/3/6 month. Some versions of it is regulated and covered by SS, but it's expensive if you going to by it without insurance coverage.
3) Bicalutamide pills - exist on the market, but not usual choice of provider
4) Spironolactone - exist on the market, but not usual choice of provider
   

# Progesterone

Exist in two formats gel capsule and gel for topical use.

1) SEIDIGESTAN gel capsule - exist in dosage 100mg and 200mg. Version with 100mg in small packs is covered by SS
2) Progeffik gel capsule - exist in dosage 100mg and 200mg. Version with 100mg in small packs is covered by SS
3) Darstin topical gel - **not** covered by SS
