# About the author and the project

Hi, my name is Clara Minova.
I'm a trans woman and an asylum seeker in Spain.
I've been living here for more than 2 years.

I decided to build this project to help trans people who have come to Spain and here I trying to answer in most common questions which can appear in mind of trans person living in Spain.
