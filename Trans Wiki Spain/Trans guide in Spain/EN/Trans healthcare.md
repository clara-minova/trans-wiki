# Types of access to trans healthcare
There is 3 ways to access to trans healthcare in Spain
1) Via public system, by using "tarjeta sanitaria" (SIP) covered by social security ("**SS**" will be used as short definition for **social security**)
2) Via private insurance
3) Completely private with appointments paid out of pocket

## Public system
In Spain each province has it's own public healthcare system. As result of that conditions and quality of access can variate.
Over all there is 3 type of trans healthcare in SS:
1) Psychological help and assessment
2) Pharmacotherapy (HRT, and/or psychopharmacological help). More info about HRT can be found at [[HRT]]
3) Surgical care (SRS, mastectomy, breast augmentation)
Often this types can be presented as steps, but in reality some steps can be skipped, especially if you not starting transition, but just continuing it after moving to Spain, or inside Spain.

### Psychological help and assessment
Usually considered as first step to access to rest of the trans healthcare.  But often can be skipped, especially if you already on HRT. Time to get it can variate a lot, because psychiatric system in SS over all considered extremely overload in Spain.

### Surgical care
Surgical care can vary a lot depending on province.
This includes what is covered/provided by SS, and how much time you need to be observed by endocrinologist in system before you will be able to get referral for surgery.

Usually you need to be observed by trans specific endocrinologist from your SS, for at least a year before you going to be able to receive referral for surgical care. After that you need to wait in waitlist. Amount of time needed to wait to receive surgical care after you got in waitlist usually at least a year, but in reality in some places this list is long as 8 years. In Barcelona waiting time is often described as 2-5 years.

But there is aways exceptions, for example if you already had surgery, and at some point of life faced complications sometimes you can get surgery much sooner. Same exceptions can apply in cases if non surgical transition (HRT/lifestyle) caused complications which requires surgical intervention. This happens because such treatment can be considered not as trans affirming care, but as ... (idk how to call when it's just medically required, despite trans status)

#### Types of trans affirming surgical care
1) Vaginoplasty and Orchiectomy
2) Mastectomy
3) Hysterectomy and adnexectomy
4) Phalloplasty
5) Breast Augmentation
6) Facial Feminization Surgery
7) Voice Feminization/Masculinization Surgery


This is the list of surgeries which is more or less exist in the system. In reality not all of them is available in every province. But Vaginoplasty, Mastectomy, Hysterectomy, Breast Augmentation usually is "available" in every province.
Even if they available in the system it's not mean that you will get access, for example for "Breast Augmentation" you need to pass into specific criteria about your breast development and size. Rules can variate and applied differently depending on you doctor(s).


### Access flow
In Spain, the procedure to follow in matters of transition depends on the specific place and case, but usually it's looks like one of this options:
- Family doctor -> Gender/Trans Unit (trans specific psychiatrist and/or endocrinologist)
- Family doctor -> Endocrinologist (general) -> Gender/Trans Unit
- Family doctor -> Psychiatrist -> Gender/Trans Unit -> Trans specific endocrinologist
- Psychiatrist ->  Gender/Trans Unit → Endocrinologist
- In some provinces you can contact the Gender/Trans Unit directly without going through your family doctor (for example in Barcelona).

In different hospitals and provinces Gender/Trans Unit can have different internal flows and protocols, and they can apply them differently depending on patient history, status, and situation. In some units, they have their own internal coordinators who decide what priority you have and what kind of doctor you need to go to first, while in other units you Family doctor can refer you not just to the unit but to a specific type of doctor directly.

Also sometimes regular (outside of Gender/Trans Unit, but still in SS) endocrinologist can be your main and only trans healthcare provider, but a regular endocrinologist usually can't put you on the waitlist for gender affirming surgeries.




## Private system

### Psychological help and assessment
Usually not required to get access to HRT and most of surgical care. If needed it's often covered covered by private insurances. Terms of conditions can variate depending on plan.

### HRT
About HRT you can read in [[HRT]]

### Surgical care
Out of pocket access is mostly straight forward, waiting time mainly depend on depends on you choose of healthcare provider. For most of surgeries certificate for from psychiatrist is NOT required. But clinics/surgeons still remain right to request such certificate.

Spanish private insurances almost always explicitly **exclude** gender affirming surgeries out of coverage. Some exceptions can be found, but it's extremely uncommon.
